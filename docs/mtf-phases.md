# MTF Phases — Summary

**HTF objective › MTF POIs › LTF execution**
Day trading: **H4** (narrative) › **M15** (bias + POIs) › **M5** (entry)

Always ask:
- **What's the current objective?**
- **What has been mitigated?**

Use the HTF to decide how aggressive you are.

---

## The two questions behind every phase

| | With the trend | Against the trend |
|---|---|---|
| **M15 swing trend** | Pro swing | Counter swing |
| **M15 internal trend** | Pro internal | Counter internal |

Each phase combines one answer from each row.

---

## A — Pro swing + Pro internal
**Everything is aligned.**

- **Situation:** the swing pullback is over, and the internal structure has turned back in the swing direction.
- **Objective:** target the **weak swing high/low**, riding the swing trend.
- **Entry:** follow the internal structure, and go straight to the M5 entry on the M15 POI.
- **No need** to wait for an M15 CHoCH at the POI.
- 👉 The cleanest phase. **Start here.**

## B — Pro swing + Counter internal
**Anticipate the end of the swing pullback.**

- **Situation:** the swing pullback is probably finishing, but the internal structure still points the other way.
- **Best when:**
  - price has pulled back **past the swing EQ** (into discount for longs, premium for shorts)
  - an **extreme M15/H4 POI** has been mitigated
- **Objective:** catch the swing turning back, targeting internal S&D or the strong high/low.
- You can wait for an M15 CHoCH, especially if an unmitigated POI sits just behind.
- If the H4 suggests the M15 could reverse, **wait for an M15 i-BOS** (which brings you back to phase A).

## C — Counter swing + Pro internal
**Trade the swing pullback.**

- **Situation:** the swing pullback has started, and the internal structure has flipped against the swing.
- **Best when:**
  - an H4 POI has been mitigated
  - a swing BOS has just printed
  - the pullback is just starting, **before the swing EQ**
- **Objective:** ride the pullback toward the EQ or the discount/premium POI. Or anticipate a swing reversal if the H4 suggests one.
- You can wait for an M15 CHoCH.

## D — Counter swing + Counter internal
**Very aggressive. Avoid until you are consistently profitable.**

- **Situation:** you trade the small internal pullback **against everything**.
- **Best when:**
  - right after a swing BOS, when a pullback is expected
  - after a refined M15 POI is mitigated
  - after a strong liquidity grab
  - the H4 trend supports it
- **Avoid** right after a swing pullback has just ended.
- 👉 Either **don't trade it**, or at least **wait for an M5 CHoCH**.

## Range
**No clear M15/H4 intention.**

- **Stay out of the middle.**
- Only **long in the bottom 33%** and **short in the top 33%**.
- Or wait for the breakout.

## Quick cheat sheet

| Phase | Swing | Internal | What you do | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | ✅ with | ✅ with | Target weak H/L | Low |
| **B** | ✅ with | ❌ against | Buy/sell the end of the pullback | Medium |
| **C** | ❌ against | ✅ with | Ride the pullback to EQ / POI | Medium |
| **D** | ❌ against | ❌ against | Scalp the internal pullback | **High** |
| **Range** | — | — | Extremes only, or wait | — |

## Golden rules
1. **Trade from strong structure, target weak structure.**
2. **Don't buy weak lows. Be cautious with shorts in swing discount.**
3. **Wait for a POI mitigation** before looking for an entry. Otherwise, wait for a CHoCH or i-BOS to form a fresh POI.
4. **When aligned with the H4, be more aggressive. Against the H4, reduce size or wait.**
